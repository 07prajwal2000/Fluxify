# Fluxify Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for contributing to Fluxify.

This agreement clarifies the intellectual property licence granted with
contributions from any person or entity. It protects you as a contributor and
protects everyone who relies on the project.

**You keep ownership of your contribution.** This agreement does not transfer
copyright. It grants a licence, so the project can distribute your work and make
licensing decisions without having to track down every past contributor
individually.

This document is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual
Contributor License Agreement v2.0.

---

## How this is accepted

There is nothing to sign. No bot, no comment, no email, no paperwork.

**By submitting a contribution to this project, you agree to the terms below.**
Opening a pull request, pushing a commit, or otherwise submitting work for
inclusion is your acceptance. This applies to your first contribution and to
every one after it.

## Contributing on behalf of a company

If you are contributing as part of your employment, please make sure your
employer is aware and has approved it. Have your employer contact the
maintainers if a Corporate CLA is required — open a
[discussion](https://github.com/Fluxify-rest/Fluxify/discussions) and we'll
sort it out.
